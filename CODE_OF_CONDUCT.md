# Code of conduct

DrainTracer is a collaborative project. People of all backgrounds and levels
of experience are welcome in its issues, pull requests, reviews, and other
project spaces.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and specific. Critique an idea or a change without attacking
  the person who proposed it.
- Make room for questions and explain technical decisions clearly.
- Accept corrections, credit others' work, and respect a contributor's time.
- Respect privacy. Ask before sharing someone's personal information or
  private correspondence.

Harassment, discriminatory remarks, sexualized attention, threats, personal
attacks, doxxing, and repeated unwanted contact are not acceptable. Neither
are deliberately disruptive participation or retaliation against someone
who reports a concern.

## Reporting a concern

The repository maintainer is
[Junaid Ahamed](https://github.com/JunaidAhamed-7777). Use a contact method
the maintainer has made public on that profile for private conduct reports.
If no suitable private route is available, open an
[issue](https://github.com/JunaidAhamed-7777/DrainTracer/issues/new) titled
"Private contact requested" with only a request for a private reporting
channel. Do not include the allegation, names, or other sensitive details
in a public issue.

Include the relevant context and links in the private report when possible.
Maintainers should handle reports discreetly and share information only as
needed to investigate or address the concern. Confidentiality cannot be
guaranteed on a public platform.

The [privacy policy](PRIVACY.md#5-information-you-send-to-the-project) describes
how voluntarily submitted information is handled. The
[legal contact guide](LEGAL.md#contact) also covers privacy and rights
requests. Report only the information needed to explain a concern; a public
request for a private channel should not identify affected people.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, give a warning,
restrict participation temporarily, or block a participant, depending on the
severity and pattern of behavior. They should explain the reason when doing
so is appropriate and safe. This policy applies to project spaces and to
people explicitly representing DrainTracer in public.
